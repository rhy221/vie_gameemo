================================================================================
METRICS
================================================================================
Quantitative (Stage 4):
  - Accuracy, Weighted-F1 (WAF), Macro-F1, UAR (Unweighted Average Recall)
  - Per-class F1
  - Confusion matrix

Reasoning (Stage 5):
  - Clue Overlap, Label Overlap scores (EMER-style)
  - Human eval Likert 1-5: chính xác, thuyết phục, chi tiết
  - Cohen's κ giữa 3 reviewer

Generalization:
  - In-distribution: Vie-GameEmo test split
  - Out-of-distribution: clips từ streamer chưa thấy trong train
  - Cross-genre: train MOBA+FPS, test Horror+RPG

Efficiency:
  - Inference time per clip (batch và real-time)
  - VRAM peak
  - Training time mỗi stage
